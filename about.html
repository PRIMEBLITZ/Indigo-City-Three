About Indigo City

Building Better Transport for Everyone

Indigo City is a smart city project that focuses on making public transport easier, fairer, and more accessible for different types of passengers.

Our Smart Hybrid Transport System combines digital services with physical options, so passengers can choose the method that works best for them.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a public transport system that is simple, accessible, and convenient for everyone.

We aim to support both digital and physical transport options so that passengers can choose the method that suits their needs.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a modern and inclusive transport system where technology improves the experience without leaving people behind.

We want Indigo City to provide reliable transport services for elderly people, people with disabilities, low-income passengers, and other members of the community.

Our Core Values

Our project focuses on creating transport that is accessible, practical, and useful for the whole community.

Innovation

We use smart technology to make public transport more convenient and efficient.

Accessibility

We support different payment and service options so more people can use public transport comfortably.

Community

We consider the needs of different passengers and work toward a transport system that benefits the whole community.

INDIGO CITY 3 is an educational research initiative highlighting the core challenges and solutions in modern public transit. We present strategies for building inclusive, hybrid digital transport systems that accommodate all passengers, including seniors and people with disabilities.
