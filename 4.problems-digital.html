PROBLEM 4

Going completely digital can create a digital divide.

Digital services can improve transport, but making them the only option can exclude passengers who cannot easily use them.

The challenge

Not every passenger has the same device, internet access, digital skills or confidence. A digital-only system can turn these differences into barriers.

No suitable device

Some passengers may not have a smartphone or device suitable for digital tickets.

Network & battery

Internet problems, technical failures or a dead phone battery can prevent access to digital services.

Digital skills

Some passengers may not feel confident using apps, QR codes or online payment.

Less human support

Removing physical service points can make it harder for passengers to receive help.

The key issue

Technology should improve transport without excluding people who cannot easily use digital services.

Digital should be an option — not the only option.
