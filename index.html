Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Explore our core FAQs below.

What is the main transport problem in Indigo?

Some passengers may have difficulty using digital transport services, while others may face accessibility problems.

Why does Indigo need a hybrid transport system?

A hybrid system gives passengers both digital and physical options, so they can choose the service that works best for them.

How can elderly passengers be supported?

Physical tickets, cash payments, smart cards and staffed service points can help elderly passengers who may struggle with the digital systems.

How can people with disabilities be supported?

Accessible spaces, suitable vehicle support and practical assistance can make public transport easier and safer to use.
