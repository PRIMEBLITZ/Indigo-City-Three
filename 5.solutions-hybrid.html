SOLUTION 1

One system. Two choices.

Indigo can combine digital and physical transport services, allowing passengers to choose the option that works best for them.

Our hybrid idea

Instead of replacing physical services completely, Indigo can keep both systems working together.

Digital transport

Mobile tickets, QR codes, digital payment and online information make travel convenient.

Physical transport

Cash, smart cards, physical tickets and staffed service points remain available.

How it works

STEP 1

Choose

Passengers choose digital or physical service.

STEP 2

Travel

Both options connect to the same transport network.

STEP 3

Get help

Staff support remains available when needed.

Who does it help?

Elderly passengers People with disabilities LIP passengers Low-income passengers
