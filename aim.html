Our Aim

Better Transport for Everyone

Our aim is to create an inclusive public transport system in Indigo by combining digital and physical services. This will give passengers more choices and make transport easier to access.

Explore Our Aim

Our Main Goals

We want Indigo's transport system to be accessible, flexible and easy for different groups of passengers.

Accessibility

Make public transport easier to use for people with disabilities and different mobility needs.

Inclusion

Support elderly passengers, people with disabilities, LIP passengers and low-income passengers.

Digital Access

Provide useful digital information and services for passengers who can use technology.

Physical Choice

Keep physical payment, tickets and staffed services for passengers who need or prefer them.

How We Plan to Help

Our approach combines technology with physical support instead of depending on only one system.

Step 01

Identify Barriers

Understand the transport difficulties faced by different passengers.

Step 02

Provide Choices

Offer both digital and physical transport services so passengers can choose what suits them.

Step 03

Improve Access

Add accessible spaces, helpful staff and clear transport information.

Hybrid System
Physical

One System, Two Choices

The hybrid system connects digital and physical transport services. Passengers can use technology when it is convenient or choose physical services when they need them.

  • Digital services: Online transport information, digital tickets and digital payment.
  • Physical services: Cash, physical tickets, smart cards and staffed service points.
  • Accessibility: Suitable spaces and practical support for passengers with disabilities.

What We Want to Improve?

Our aim is to make transport more accessible and easier for different groups of passengers.

1

Accessibility

Reduce barriers for people with disabilities.

2

Choice

Give passengers both digital and physical options.

3

Inclusion

Support different passengers in using public transport.

Building a More Inclusive Indigo

By combining digital technology with physical support, Indigo can create a public transport system that gives more people a fair and practical choice.
