SOLUTION 2

Design transport for different abilities.

Indigo can improve accessibility by designing vehicles, stops and services around different passenger needs.

Accessibility by design

Accessibility should cover the complete journey — reaching the stop, boarding, travelling and leaving the vehicle.

Wheelchair spaces

Provide suitable spaces so wheelchair users can travel safely and comfortably.

Ramps & low floors

Easier boarding can support passengers with reduced mobility.

Priority seating

Clearly marked priority seats can support passengers who need them.

Trained staff

Drivers and staff can provide respectful assistance when required.

Accessible journey

STEP 1

Reach

Make transport stops easier to access.

STEP 2

Board

Provide accessible boarding facilities.

STEP 3

Travel

Provide safe space and support.

Main beneficiaries

Wheelchair users Older people People with reduced mobility People with disabilities
